# Commercial direction

**Local AddonRelay tools, supported local Workbench use and basic linked-project monitoring are intended to remain free.** The intended product includes a headless agent connection, supporting terminal tools and an optional browser project dashboard. Monitoring will have published service limits; free does not mean unlimited hosted storage. A required subscription, timed trial, hosted account, or API key is not part of the local product direction.

Users supply their own supported AI client and any model-provider access it requires. Those providers' usage charges or limits are separate from AddonRelay.

The planned dashboard separates free local activity from paid hosted usage. Viewing activity, synchronizing an approved local result and reconnecting must not silently consume credits. Its credit view will show AddonRelay balances, reservations and actual charges, not an AI provider’s subscription balance.

## Planned paid offering

Optional hosted investigations will be considered first through prepaid usage, with the price or accepted maximum shown before authorized paid work. Local checks, viewing an existing result, refreshing the dashboard and reconnecting are not billable investigations.

An optional monthly plan with included hosted usage may follow once recurring customer value, delivery cost and capacity have been measured. Prepaid use should remain available for occasional creators. No subscription price, credit conversion, included allowance, automatic refill or timed trial is announced here. AddonRelay should not impose surprise overage charges.

New public account/dashboard access and billing remain closed. Monitoring access will be qualified separately from paid processing and checkout. Public rates, purchase availability, or a hosted Brain service are not announced by this roadmap. Any future subscription will describe what it provides and how cancellation and remaining purchased credits are handled before it is offered.

Implementation source and the detailed engineering plan are maintained privately. Free use of a future distributed product is a product direction, not an open-source license or a new legal grant. The existing [rights notice](../../NOTICE.md) applies until the applicable distribution terms are selected and published.

No third-party engine, addon, provider, or game-asset rights are included merely because AddonRelay supports an integration.
