# Commercial direction

**Local AddonRelay tools and supported local Workbench use are intended to remain free.** The intended product includes a headless agent connection, supporting terminal tools and an optional browser project dashboard. A required subscription, timed trial, hosted account, or API key is not part of that local product direction.

Users supply their own supported AI client and any model-provider access it requires. Those providers' usage charges or limits are separate from AddonRelay.

The planned dashboard separates free local activity from paid hosted usage. Viewing activity, synchronizing an approved local result and reconnecting must not silently consume credits. Its credit view will show AddonRelay balances, reservations and actual charges, not an AI provider’s subscription balance.

Optional hosted investigations may be offered later through prepaid usage. New public account/dashboard access and billing remain closed. Monitoring access will be qualified separately from paid processing and checkout. Public rates, purchase availability, or a hosted Brain service are not announced by this roadmap.

Implementation source and the detailed engineering plan are maintained privately. Free use of a future distributed product is a product direction, not an open-source license or a new legal grant. The existing [rights notice](../../NOTICE.md) applies until the applicable distribution terms are selected and published.

No third-party engine, addon, provider, or game-asset rights are included merely because AddonRelay supports an integration.
